BEEDO-TRADE: IMPORTANT NOTICE

Beedo-Trade is a learning and decision-support tool. It applies the Beedo technical-analysis course method to Egyptian Exchange (EGX) stocks.

- It is NOT investment advice and does not know your full financial situation.
- It never places orders. You make every decision and trade in your own broker app.
- Prices come from free sources (Yahoo Finance) and can be delayed or missing. Always check the live price and the day's EGX disclosures before trading.
- Trading shares involves risk, including the loss of money you invest.

By installing, you confirm that you understand this.
