        About Beedo-Trade

        Beedo-Trade applies the Beedo technical-analysis course to Egyptian Exchange stocks, the same way every time: 12 indicators (the 11 course indicators plus MACD), one signal, and separate advice for a cash holder and a shareholder.

        The signal ladder

        Alarm = early warning, get ready. First = start a position (or start reducing). Confirm = several signals agree, act fully. The signal is the score of the ticked indicators: each adds its rung (Confirm +3, First +2, Alarm +1, Hold 0, minus for sells); 9 or more = Confirm, 5 = First, 2 = Alarm.

        Where the numbers come from

        • Prices: daily history from Yahoo Finance (e.g. COMI → COMI.CA), and the last two trading days from TradingView, 15 minutes delayed (during trading hours, today so far). The larger, widely traded EGX stocks are covered; for others, use Import CSV with date, open, high, low, close and volume columns.
        • Indicators: moving averages you choose (default 5 / 9 / 18, the course's own), MACD 12 / 26 / 9, RSI 14 (Wilder), swing highs and lows, support and resistance, 20-day volume average, reversal candles and gaps.
        • Chart and pattern indicators 10 and 11 are judged on the chart: the swing points are marked for you.
        • Company fundamentals: TradingView's market data (last 12 months of reported results), rated with standard rules and compared with similar companies. Smaller companies often have too few reported figures to rate.

        Important

        This is a learning and decision-support tool. It is not investment advice, it does not know your full financial situation, and it never places orders. Free data can be delayed: always check the live price and the day's disclosures before trading.
