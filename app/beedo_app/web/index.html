        About Beedo-Trade

        Beedo-Trade applies the Beedo technical-analysis course to Egyptian Exchange stocks, the same way every time: 11 steps, one signal, and separate advice for a cash holder and a shareholder.

        The signal ladder

        Alarm = early warning, get ready. First = start a position (or start reducing). Confirm = several signals agree, act fully. Trend and moving averages set the ceiling: candles and RSI alone can't confirm a move against them, and in a sideways market the cash holder waits for a breakout.

        Where the numbers come from

        • Daily prices: Yahoo Finance (e.g. COMI → COMI.CA). The larger, widely traded EGX stocks are covered; for others, use Import CSV with date, open, high, low, close and volume columns.
        • Indicators: MA 5 / 9 / 18, RSI 14 (Wilder), swing highs and lows, support and resistance, 20-day volume average, reversal candles and gaps.
        • Chart and pattern steps 10 and 11 are judged on the chart: the swing points are marked for you.
        • Company fundamentals: TradingView's market data (last 12 months of reported results), rated with standard rules and compared with similar companies. Smaller companies often have too few reported figures to rate.

        Important

        This is a learning and decision-support tool. It is not investment advice, it does not know your full financial situation, and it never places orders. Free data can be delayed: always check the live price and the day's disclosures before trading.
