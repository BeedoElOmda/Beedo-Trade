## 1. Core principles
- Market action discounts everything; prices move in trends; history repeats (crowd psychology: greed buys, fear sells).
- **"We follow technique, not price."** A cheap price is not a good buy and a high price is not a bad buy. Target the right *position* to buy or sell, not the price.
- Trader discipline: know yourself, make your plan, control your risk, patience, humbleness.
- Information drives trends. EGX disclosures (الافصاحات) come out before the 10:00 a.m. open, so check news before acting on a signal.
- Do not buy near resistance. If price breaks a resistance and the next resistance is far: cash holder buys, shareholder holds.

## 2. Signal ladder and the two investor roles
Every step of the analysis lands on one rung:

| Rung | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Alarm Buy / Alarm Sell | Early warning that momentum may be turning | Do nothing yet; get ready |
| First Buy / First Sell | Initial entry; turn started but not confirmed | Start a position (or start reducing) |
| Confirm Buy / Confirm Sell | Turn is real; several signals agree | Full buy / full sell |
| Hold | No clear signal, or conflicting signals | Stay as you are |

Two roles always get separate advice:
- **Cash holder**: has cash, does not own the stock.
- **Shareholder**: already owns the stock.

## 3. Trend
- Trend is identified by **2 highs and 2 lows**; **confirmed by the 3rd high and 3rd low**.
- Uptrend = higher highs + higher lows (buyers command). Downtrend = lower highs + lower lows (sellers command). Sideways = buyers equal sellers.
- Strong trend: steady moves, small pullbacks, volume rises on rallies, MAs slope steeply.
- **Danger uptrend**: a rise with *no correction*; risky to chase.

| Trend | Shareholder | Cash holder |
|---|---|---|
| Uptrend | Hold (or buy more) | Buy at 2H/2L, Confirm Buy at 3rd H/L |
| Downtrend | Sell at 2H/2L, Confirm Sell at 3rd H/L | Hold cash (stay out) |
| Sideways | Sell / reduce (out of market for other opportunities) | Hold cash (stay out) |

Uptrend sequence: Buy (breakout from consolidation or resistance on higher volume) → Confirm Buy (new higher high and higher low) → Hold while volume and MAs support it.
Downtrend confirmation signs: price breaks support, volume above average, MAs cross down, price fails to climb back above the broken support.

## 4. Support, resistance and pricing zones
- Support (الدعم): level below price where demand exceeds supply. Resistance (المقاومة): level above price where supply exceeds demand.
- Draw lines on the turning points (between open and close). Use both **historical** (major, long-term) and **recent** levels.

| Price zone | Cash holder | Shareholder | What to look for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before support | Hold and watch; no buy until candle confirmation | Hold; prepare First Sell if support breaks | Hammer / bullish engulfing, RSI 30→50, volume spike |
| At support (testing) | Prepare to buy if confirmed; wait for rejection wick | Hold or add small | Hammer/engulfing + volume + RSI up from 30. **Best risk-reward buy zone, if confirmed.** |
| Below support (breakdown) | Do NOT buy; wait for retest that fails | First Sell or Confirm Sell; protect with stop-loss | Retest fails → Sell. No long positions |
| Before resistance | Hold and watch | Hold; watch break or rejection | Avoid buying high without a breakout |
| At resistance (testing) | Do NOT buy; wait for breakout and retest | Hold; take partial profit if bearish signs | Shooting star, bearish engulfing, RSI 70→50 |
| Below resistance after a failed breakout | Avoid buying | First Sell (failed breakout) | RSI divergence strengthens the sell |

## 5. Moving averages (5 / 9 / 18-day system)
- Price above MA = uptrend: no sell recommendation (buy or hold, depending on nearby S/R). Price below MA = downtrend: no buy recommendation.
- MA sloping up = uptrend; down = downtrend; flat = sideways.
- Shorter MA = faster, closer to price, more trades, higher risk.

| Cross | Up = | Down = |
|---|---|---|
| MA5 crosses MA9 | **Alarm Buy** (also called Golden Cross in the course) | **Alarm Sell** (Death Cross) |
| MA5 crosses MA18 | **First Buy** | **First Sell** |
| MA9 crosses MA18 | **Confirm Buy**: act | **Confirm Sell**: act |

- Full uptrend order: MA5 > MA9 > MA18. Downtrend order: MA5 < MA9 < MA18. Mixed order during corrections/consolidations is normal ("intermingling"); the main trend can still be intact.
- When the slow and fast MAs cross *and* price breaks resistance → Confirm Buy (with no reversal candles). Cross + support break → Confirm Sell.

## 6. Volume
| Price | Volume | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Up | Up | Strong buying, uptrend likely continues |
| Up | Down | Fewer buyers; rise likely to stall |
| Down | Up | Strong selling, downtrend likely continues |
| Down | Down | Selling fading; possible bottom / reversal up |

Volume rising = trend strength; falling = consolidation; spike = breakout/breakdown. Breakouts need a volume spike; a weak-volume breakout risks being false.

## 7. Reversal candles (always need the next candle to confirm)
| Candle | Shape | Where | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hammer | Small body, lower shadow ≥ 2× body, little upper shadow | Bottom of downtrend | Bullish reversal; buy if next candle closes higher |
| Inverted Hammer | Small body, upper shadow ≥ 2× body | Bottom of downtrend | Bullish reversal if confirmed |
| Hanging Man | Hammer shape | Top of uptrend | Bearish reversal; next candle lower confirms |
| Shooting Star | Inverted-hammer shape | Top of uptrend | Bearish; next close below its body confirms |
| Doji | Open ≈ close | Anywhere | Indecision: at top → may turn down; at bottom → may turn up; mid-range → neutral |

Rule of thumb: at the bottom think "sellers losing control" (bullish); at the top think "buyers losing control" (bearish).

## 8. RSI (14-day, Wilder)
| RSI | Condition | Shareholder | Cash holder |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≥ 70 | Overbought, possible downtrend soon | Alarm to Sell | Hold |
| Falling 70 → 50 | Bearish momentum | First Sell | Hold |
| Falling 50 → 30 | Downtrend confirmed | Confirm Sell | Hold |
| = 50 | Neutral | Hold | Hold |
| Rising 30 → 50 | Trend reversal up | Hold | First Buy |
| Rising 50 → 70 | Bullish momentum, uptrend confirmed | Hold | Confirm Buy |
| ≤ 30 | Oversold, possible uptrend soon | Alarm to Buy | Hold |

- In strong bull markets, 80 acts as overbought; in bear markets, 20 acts as oversold. Overbought can last in a strong uptrend, so it is not by itself a reason to sell.
- The first move into an extreme zone is a warning; watch the second move.
- **Divergence** (most important RSI signal, strongest above 70 / below 30):
  - Negative (bearish): price higher high, RSI lower high → uptrend weak → Hold (maybe sell); First Sell after candle confirmation.
  - Positive (bullish): price lower low, RSI higher low → downtrend weak → Hold (maybe buy); First Buy after candle confirmation.
- RSI shows momentum strength, not trend.

## 9. Price gaps
Check first whether a gap is due to a dividend or corporate action; that is not a market signal.

| Gap | Where | Volume | Fill | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breakaway | Start of new trend, after sideways / at S/R break | High | Rarely filled | Gap up breaking resistance → Buy; gap down breaking support → Sell |
| Continuation (runaway / measuring) | Middle of strong trend | Moderate-high, rising | Not filled in next 3-4 days | Uptrend → Buy/Hold; downtrend → Sell/Hold. Remaining move ≈ distance already travelled |
| Exhaustion | Near end of long trend | Very high | Filled quickly | Uptrend → Alarm Sell; downtrend → Alarm Buy |
| Island reversal | Gap, short isolated range, opposite gap | High | Island stays isolated | Gap up → island → gap down = **Strong Sell**; gap down → island → gap up = **Strong Buy** |

A close below an upward gap is a sign of weakness.

## 10. Reversal patterns
For all: **First signal = break of the neckline (with volume); Confirm = retest fails to move back across the neckline.**

| Pattern | After | Signal | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Head & Shoulders | Uptrend | Sell | Highest left shoulder, lower head, lowest right shoulder |
| Double Top / Triple Top | Uptrend | Sell (triple stronger) | Each later peak on lower volume |
| Inverse H&S | Downtrend | Buy | Volume rises on breakout above neckline |
| Double / Triple Bottom | Downtrend | Buy (triple stronger) | Each later low on lower volume |

## 11. Continuation patterns
Sideways balance inside a trend. Strategy while inside: **Hold**.

| Pattern | Bias | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Ascending triangle (flat top, rising lows) | Bullish | First Buy on breakout above resistance with high volume; Confirm Buy when retest holds above old resistance |
| Descending triangle (flat bottom, falling highs) | Bearish | First Sell on breakdown below support with high volume; Confirm Sell when retest stays below |
| Symmetrical triangle (lower highs + higher lows) | Usually continues prior trend | First Buy/Sell on break in trend direction; Confirm on retest. Weak volume = false-break risk |
