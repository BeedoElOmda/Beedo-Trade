# Beedo-Trade privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Applies to the Beedo-Trade apps for Android, Windows and Mac.

## In short

Beedo-Trade has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

Your watchlist, alerts, portfolio (stock codes, quantities and prices you enter), cash and risk settings, moving averages, ticked checklist steps, language and theme are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. The app also keeps your last eight analyses there so they can be shown without internet. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## What the app sends over the internet

To show prices and analyses, the app requests public market data from these services. The request contains the stock code (for example COMI) and nothing about you. As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address.

| Service | What for |
|---|---|
| Yahoo Finance | Daily prices of EGX stocks, gold, silver and the dollar rate |
| TradingView | Company figures, market health and the latest values for stocks without price history |
| GitHub | Checking whether a newer version of Beedo-Trade exists (desktop apps) |

Their own privacy policies apply to those requests. Beedo-Trade does not send them your watchlist, portfolio or any personal information.

## Notifications (Android)

If you allow notifications, the app checks your alerts on the phone itself once a day after the market closes and shows the result as a notification. The check runs on your phone; nothing is sent to the developer.

## Permissions (Android)

- **Internet**: to download market data.
- **Notifications**: to show your alerts. You can switch this off in Android's settings at any time.

## Children

Beedo-Trade is meant for adults learning about stock analysis. It is not directed at children.

## Not investment advice

Beedo-Trade is a learning and decision-support tool. It does not give personal financial advice and never places orders.
